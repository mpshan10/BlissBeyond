Who We Are

A modern way to plan meaningful events

Bliss & Beyond is an event planning and catering platform created to make celebrations easier to organize, personalize and manage. Our goal is to provide one clear and convenient place where customers can begin turning an event idea into a complete celebration.

Instead of searching separately for venues, catering, decoration, flowers, photography, entertainment and other requirements, users can explore these important parts through one connected platform.

We support weddings, birthdays, engagements, anniversaries, graduations and corporate celebrations while giving users the flexibility to select services that match their preferences, requirements and event style.

Everything in One Place

Explore venues, packages and individual event services without managing every requirement separately.

Personalized Planning

Choose services and details that match your preferences instead of relying only on fixed event combinations.

Greater Convenience

Keep important event choices connected and easier to understand, review and organize.
