About Our Events FAQ

Find answers to the most common questions about planning your special event with Bliss & Beyond.

Bliss & Beyond can help plan weddings, engagement celebrations, birthdays, graduations, corporate events, anniversaries, private celebrations, and other special occasions. Available venues, packages, and services depend on the event details and customer selections.

We recommend booking as early as possible, especially for weekends and popular dates. Larger events such as weddings and corporate functions usually require more planning time, while smaller celebrations may be arranged with a shorter lead time depending on venue and service availability.

Yes. Customers can build a custom event by selecting an available venue and choosing services such as catering, decoration and styling, photography and videography, entertainment, lighting and AV, cake and desserts, and floral design.

Package inclusions vary by package. A package may include a venue and selected event services such as catering, decoration, photography, entertainment, lighting and AV, cake and desserts, and floral design. The package detail page shows the exact inclusions before booking.

For a custom booking, enter your event date, start time, end time, and guest count on the Venues page. Bliss & Beyond will show venues that match the availability and capacity requirements.

Guest count changes may be possible before the event is finalized, but changes can affect venue suitability, catering quantities, service pricing, and the overall quotation. Customers should update the event details as early as possible.

If a venue is unavailable for the selected date and time, the system will not present it as available for that booking period. Customers can adjust the event date or time or choose another available venue.

Eligible bookings can be cancelled through the customer booking area according to the current booking status and cancellation rules. Cancelled bookings remain visible in Booking History for reference.

Pricing depends on the selected venue, guest count, services, package choices, and service customisations. The booking and quotation flow shows the relevant estimated or calculated prices before final confirmation.

Yes. Customers can choose a ready-made package from the Packages page or use Start Custom Booking to build an event by selecting a venue and services.

Available services include Catering, Decoration & Styling, Photography & Videography, Entertainment, Lighting & AV, Cake & Desserts, and Floral Design.

Customers can book a consultation or send an enquiry through Bliss & Beyond for additional help with event planning and service selection.
